## Premise

Caleb and Nora Bennett, fraternal twins a few months past eighteen, grew up on a family
story about their father: Dr. Daniel Mercer vanished before they were born, seven or eight
weeks into Rachel Bennett's pregnancy, before the first ultrasound. He never knew there
were two of them. A car crash gives both twins the same impossible vision at once, and
each keeps different details of it: Nora the spatial, structural, technical shape of the
thing, Caleb the human, physical, sounded parts. Fitting the halves together starts an
investigation that turns the family's official story inside out. Daniel's research into
scale manipulation was real, it was erased on purpose, and someone is still watching for
his name.

The trail leads to an island that cannot be found by ordinary means, where humans live
at insect scale, seven to ten millimetres tall, in a settlement dug ninety percent
underground behind a shield that keeps its surface doors stable. The people there
adapted to an ecosystem they never conquered: ants managed at colony level, beetles
bonded and ridden, mantises barely conditioned, wolf spiders tolerated by a rare few,
and a bestiary beyond that which stays permanently dangerous. Daniel lived there. He
saved these people and, in one decision, ruined some of them, and then he left. Where he
went, why, and whether he is still alive is the search that carries the series. At their
size a mile is an expedition, so learning to survive and travel beyond the shield *is*
the search. This is grounded sci-fi wearing fantasy-scale imagery, played with
RPG-adventure momentum, audiobook-first, told in close third person, one sibling at a time.

## Locked core cast (one line each)

- **Caleb Bennett** (18) — co-protagonist; retains the human, physical, sound-and-action side of the vision; gifted and self-taught, not credentialed.
- **Nora Bennett** (18) — co-protagonist; retains the spatial, technical, pattern side; gifted and self-taught, not credentialed.
- **Rachel Bennett** (40s) — their mother; competent, guarded, keeping her own secrets and running her own long game; unknown to everyone on the island.
- **Dr. Daniel Mercer** (late 40s–50s island time) — the missing father; alive, complicated, neither villain nor saint; left the settlement, current location unknown to everyone.
- **Professor Adrian Kessler** — normal-world professor, NOT Daniel; a former colleague who recognizes Daniel's technical fingerprint before any family link; gives Part One real information.
- **Dr. Elena Reyes** (early-mid 30s) — island researcher/teacher, the in-house authority on the taming/scale-state overlap; enters ~Ch. 60–65 already ~7 months pregnant; collaborated with Daniel while he was still present.
- **Dr. Maya Voss** (late 20s–early 30s) — field overwatch and coordination specialist; her signature is a bonded flying creature (species TBD), not a weapon; grows into Elena's responsibilities and stays central.
- **Theo Calder** (mid-late 30s) — settlement-born creature-handling and shield-systems specialist; Daniel's old colleague, carries guilt for the abandoned-settlement tragedy; becomes the twins' most important mentor.
- **Finn Okoro** (early-mid 20s) — scout, tracker, climber; not settlement-born, arrived via an earlier "lost" incident; peer and rival-turned-friend, closest to Caleb first; the future false spy suspect, genuinely innocent.
- **Silas Kade** (early-mid 30s) — medical/research specialist on how scale-state affects the body; lost family in the second settlement's fall; distrusts anyone connected to Daniel; slowest to warm, loyalty worth the most.

## Rules that bite every chapter

- No backward time. Regions run forward at different rates; biology follows the time a person personally lives through.
- The shield is a binary scale-stability field. Its real, constant job is protecting the settlement's surface access points, not doming a town.
- Creature progression is skill-gated, never level-gated. Domestication, taming, bonding and training are four different things. Creatures age, get hurt, revert, and die, and losses matter.
- Human gear reads primitive, handmade and scale-appropriate: blades, spears, bows, crossbows, darts, traps, rope, silk. No miniature firearms.
- Altitude gives information and altitude makes you visible.
- The island was never waiting for Caleb and Nora. It has its own grudges, routines and grief.
- Deliberately unknown, do not explain: what is under the sealed tunnel; the anomaly's origin; whether Daniel's device discovered or created the phenomenon; who all funded his research; whether there is more than one island; Daniel's exact whereabouts.
