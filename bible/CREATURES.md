# Creatures

Living canon for every species and every named individual creature. The four
distinctions are canon and never blur: **domestication** (species-level,
generational), **taming** (one animal's tolerance), **bonding** (one handler, one
animal, sociable species only), **training** (learned behaviours). Progression is
skill-gated. Creatures are living beings: they are injured, age, revert, retire,
reproduce and die, and no loss is replaced by an upgrade.

Numbers are labelled: **MEASURED** (real biology, with a source), **BIOLOGICAL
SHAPE** (true in kind, tuned in degree), or **STORY** (chosen for the story). When a
species enters the page for the first time, its entry is proposed in the handoff
report and lands here on approval.
