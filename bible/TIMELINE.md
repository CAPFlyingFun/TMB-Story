# Timeline

Two clocks, tracked separately, never merged. No backward time; regions run forward
at different rates; **biology follows the time a person personally lives through**
(Rev 2 rule 7). Every chapter's `story_time.clock` says which clock it is lived on.
Flag explicitly wherever the rates are shown to diverge.

## Fixed points (architecture)
- Daniel disappeared ~7–8 weeks into Rachel's pregnancy, before the first ultrasound; did not know about twins.
- At Chapter 1 the twins are 18, a few months past their birthday. Daniel has been gone roughly eighteen and a half years of outside time.
- Elena is ~7 months pregnant at ~Ch. 60–65; birth ~8–10 island weeks later (~Ch. 135–165), tracked by elapsed story time, with explicit time-skips.
- Maya's pregnancy: broad ~Ch. 500–600 window, staggered after Daniel's reveal.
- Real time passes between the second settlement's fall and Daniel's departure (gradual erosion, not flight).
