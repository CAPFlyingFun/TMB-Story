# Mysteries — the living ladder

Every question has an id. Chapters record ids in `mysteries.introduced/advanced/
answered`; `CHAPTER_INDEX.md` shows them as `+M##`, `~M##`, `=M##`. Part targets are
the architecture's (Rev 2 §17 as revised by Rev 3 §9) and are intentions, not
deadlines; an answer landing outside its range is a decision for
`architecture/DECISIONS.md`, not a drift. The rhythm question → clue →
investigation → answer → bigger question runs every 5–15 chapters. The ladder grows;
new questions get the next id.

**Who knows what** is kept per mystery below the table and is the field most likely to
prevent a contradiction.
