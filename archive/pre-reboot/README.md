# ARCHIVED — PRE-REBOOT — NOT CANON

**Everything in this directory is obsolete. Do not treat any of it as current canon.**

On 2026-09-17 Joshua rebooted TRADDOMIUM: Micro Battle!. The story now begins with
the TOMBS catastrophe in Chapters 1 to 3, and is already in the miniature setting by
the end of Chapter 3. Current canon is:

1. The approved manuscript in `chapters/`
2. `story-rules/` (formerly `bible/`)
3. `architecture/DECISIONS.md`

## What is in here and why it is dead

- **`chapters/part-01/`** — the pre-reboot Chapters 1 to 3. A completely different
  story: Caleb and Nora Bennett, eighteen-year-old twins, seasonal field assistants
  at Alder Sound Field Station, searching for their missing father Daniel Mercer.
  Tern Island, a gravel bar, a shore logger, a padlocked enclosure, a car crash and a
  shared vision. **None of these people, places or events are canon.** The current
  Chapter 1 is "The Alarm" and its characters are Jack and Sarah Bennett.
- **`outline/`** — the fifty-chapter plan. It spent roughly fifty chapters of ordinary
  life before reaching the miniature island. **That pacing is superseded.** Do not
  try to reconcile it with the reboot and do not continue it.
- **`bible/`** — the pre-reboot canon reference. Its characters, locations, creatures,
  timeline, chapter index and mystery ladder all describe the archived story.
- **`reviews/`** — handoff reports for the archived chapters.
- **`SERIES_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `.docx`** — the long-form series architecture, which
  is where the fifty-chapter progression and the twenty-Part roadmap came from.
- **`architecture-history/`** — earlier revisions of the same.

## What survived the reboot

The premise survived: an island, a human settlement reduced to insect scale, and a
young protagonist who has to live there. The craft rules survived and are restated in
`story-rules/TMB_STORY_RULES.md`. The Bennett surname survived, attached to different
people. Almost nothing else did.
