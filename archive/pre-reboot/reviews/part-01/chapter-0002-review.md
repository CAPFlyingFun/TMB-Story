## What the chapter does

The same Tuesday, dusk to a quarter past eleven, one location. Marco drops the twins
at the bottom of their hill at a quarter to seven with Saturday's skiff run and "wear
them wetter"; one spoken paragraph covers the station afternoon (Caleb's *ants* in
the log's box for cause; Ines sending the soft kit home to dry). In the front hall
Nora sees her mother fold a letter in three into her back pocket; Caleb, behind her,
sees dinner. The long kitchen scene carries the physical spine: boots scraped and
rinsed on the back step and stuffed with newspaper, the net bag shaken out (the dead
fly in a bee costume), gloves and net on the heater under the black window, the
two-count and "Fun one" in stereo, Caleb's telling (mud, gull, ants), Nora's numbers,
Rachel's technician's questions, the jars re-labeled in pencil with the date left
blank, and the title beat: the dried net bag on the kitchen scale, sixty-two grams
over its sheet weight, Rachel's arithmetic, and Nora calling it salt anyway. Dinner:
the photograph shown being ignored, "that twin thing", "We built it the hard way",
Caleb's "Did Dad ever work out there?", the fixed words, "Pass your brother the salt"
with the salt already at his elbow, "Caleb. Eat.", and Rachel's "I don't love you out
on that bar", which the twins explain away as the obvious thing. Repacking on the
kitchen floor; the packs by the door for Saturday. Then Nora on the silent edges of
the stairs at a quarter past eleven, and her mother in the porch light at the shelf,
keys in one hand, looking at the photograph nobody looks at, standing between the
packs and the door. Nora goes back up without the water.

## Deviations from the card and the outline, and why

- **The two-count is in the kitchen, not "in the car earlier"** (outline §2). The
  card does not fix the place; the kitchen lets Rachel overhear it, which pays.
- **"That twin thing" is sourced to "somebody at the station", not to Ines or a
  station manager.** Ines said the opposite on the page in Chapter 1 ("It was both")
  and does not waste words; a named source would be out of character or would
  create Rachel–Ines canon Chapter 7 does not need yet. Rachel's vagueness is a
  small Nora-noticing, explainable.
- **Rachel's questions are three, not one,** but only one is "too many" in kind;
  the mystery specialist's advice was to keep them all technician-shaped. Nora's
  conscious count of them was cut from the draft as too loud for Chapter 2.
- **The "rinse the sample jars" beat became re-labeling empty jars.** Chapter 1's
  page shows an identify-and-release sweep; no samples were taken, so there is
  nothing to rinse. The jars are re-labeled in pencil for Saturday instead.
- **Count sheets are not dried on the radiator** (outline). Chapter 1 sealed them in
  the dry bag; they stay dry and stay at the station.
- **The moth is unnamed on the page** ("a fat brown moth"); the bible will carry it as
  a large yellow underwing, which also seeds Mini-Arc 4's moth motif.
