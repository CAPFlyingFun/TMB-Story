# Orphaned voice clips

Audio the revised Chapters 1 to 6 no longer reference. **Nothing here is deleted**, and
nothing here is lost: a clip is addressed by its speaker and its exact spoken text, so
restoring a sentence to the manuscript makes the pipeline find its old audio again --
move the pair back beside its siblings in `audio/clips/<speaker>/` and it is cached.

Moved on 2026-09-21, when the quiet-event revision landed. 51 clips, all of them from
the pre-revision Chapters 1 to 3: the lines that changed, plus the settlement
evacuation sequence in Chapter 2 that the revision removes.
