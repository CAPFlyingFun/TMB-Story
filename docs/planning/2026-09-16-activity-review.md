# Activity review of the Part One plan under decision 0007 (2026-09-16)

Kept for the planning room (Joshua + ChatGPT) while Chapters 1–50 are re-planned
top-down on Trello. Reference, not canon. The test: does the overall adventure give
the future player meaningful opportunities to perform the same important actions
the characters performed. Do not change a clue to create gameplay; change how and
where it is obtained. Do not manufacture activity.

## Proposed Chapters 1–10 (`outline/part-01/mini-arc-01-REVISED-proposed.md`)

Strongly playable as planned: 1 (the bar), 3 (the enclosure, the crash), 7 (records
and the dock), 8 (the boxes), 10 (low water). Chapter 4 (hospital) stays a
story/cutscene chapter by design.

- **Ch. 2** — dinner only. Activity can happen while the twins clean and re-pack the
  field gear for the return trip; the photograph and the folded letter stay as they are.
- **Ch. 5** — the halves fit; stillness earned. Nora physically reconstructs and draws
  her vision; the drawing becomes evidence the story and the game both carry.
- **Ch. 6** — recovery week. Do not force gameplay; natural recovery and the 2 a.m.
  discovery of Rachel's folder are enough. One-handed struggle is characterization,
  not an objective.
- **Ch. 9** — avoid repeating Ch. 7's desk research two chapters later. Obtaining the
  annual reports should involve a place or an activity (the boathouse loft with Gus's
  keys, or a trip to Cascade State's campus that seeds Kessler's building).

## Approved Chapters 11–50 (`outline/part-01/mini-arc-02..05.md`)

- **Ch. 11–20 (Mini-Arc 2)** — needs the largest restructuring, and needs a rewrite
  anyway because the crash moved. As approved, only 12 and 15 are physical; 13, 16,
  18, 20 are desk work and 11, 14, 17, 19 are conversations. Same discoveries, reached
  by going somewhere: the scrubbed records as a campus expedition into the institute's
  old building and archive; the coordinates as a coastal trip matching the tide-table
  region against charts at the marina (brings Wes Tanaka forward); "someone was here"
  moved from the wasp nest to the Tern Island enclosure found emptied or its logger
  dead; the archivist's warning as an after-hours visit. Kessler's office hours and
  the voicemail keep their stillness.
- **Ch. 21–30 (Mini-Arc 3)** — mostly balanced. 21 (reconstructing the research) can
  happen while building the first rig; 27 (the cover-story timeline) assembled
  physically from documents in the boxes and crawlspace. Required fix: Ch. 28's
  Halloran encounter is at a boatyard that no longer exists; move it to the Alder
  Sound dock or car park.
- **Ch. 31–40 (Mini-Arc 4)** — move some research into field, water and equipment
  activity: 32 (modelling the region) as a first run on Wes's boat toward the shelf
  edge with the station's spare loggers; 34 (the buoy archive) as a decommissioned
  buoy logger physically in the Alder Sound boathouse. 36 can stay conversation.
- **Ch. 41–50 (Mini-Arc 5)** — already strongly compatible. 44's shrink is cutscene
  material; 45–50 are the game's first playable island sequence as written.
