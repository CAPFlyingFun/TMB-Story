## Voice

Close third person, past tense, as the English. Dialogue reads as people speak: Jack and
Sarah to each other *aku / kamu*; colleagues *aku / kamu* once they are working together
(Lena, Mark); Mark's first words to Jack, a stranger he calls "Doctor", *saya / Anda*.
Short lines stay short. English idioms are carried by their meaning, not their words
("Try harder." → "Berusahalah lebih keras." reads stiff; "Lebih lucu lagi, dong." keeps the
tease). Numbers are written as words, as the English writes them.
