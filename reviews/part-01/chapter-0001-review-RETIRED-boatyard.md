## What the chapter does

A drowned outboard comes into the boatyard with a story that doesn't fit the damage; Caleb finds the truth by the pull-cord and the plug, and gets it out of the man with a question instead of an accusation. At dinner the family says the fixed words about Dad, Rachel folds a letter away, and the twins answer "the twin thing" in chorus after a two-count. The chapter ends on the count being spent, again, on the wasps Caleb is never going to deal with.

## What changed from the outline and why

- **The "twin thing" question is asked at the yard and answered at dinner.** The outline had a customer receive the answer; the dinner performance for Rachel is where the ritual lands, so the yard gets Caleb's flat "Nope" and dinner gets the chorus. Both specialists who raised it preferred this.
- **The official story's fixed words are now set by the page:** *"Research trip. The boat was lost off the coast. Nobody was ever found. Eighteen years ago."* The outline said "body never recovered"; the page's "Nobody" is better aloud and does not assert he was alone (Ch. 24 reveals two people aboard). If approved, the outline (`mini-arc-01.md`, `overview.md`) should be updated to this wording, and the audio brief should tell the narrator not to split it into "no body."
- **The wasps are paper wasps** on an open comb, slow and silent in the October cool; the reason Caleb has not dealt with them is that the first frost will.
- **The letter** is one clause at Caleb's entry: a sheet of paper folded in three and pocketed before his boots are off. He assigns it nothing.
