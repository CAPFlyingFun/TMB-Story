## What changed from the card and why

- **Crabs are omitted.** The card has "crabs retreating" among the creature
  signals. The world-creature specialist's advice was that on a northern gravel
  bar in October the shore crabs are under stones and weed by day and would not be
  visible retreating; a listener would picture something the beach would not show.
  The signal is carried by the shorebirds lifting together and the ants instead,
  which the card also names. Flagging rather than adding a crab beat that the
  biology would not support.
- **The spider web beat is not spent here.** Chapter 3's card owns the web on the
  dashboard. Nothing here pre-empts it.
- **The ants react to a wet log, not to the weather.** Carpenter ants move brood
  when the nest is compromised; the drift log at the wrack line is soaked from
  underneath (the water at low was already high), so they relocate to the dry
  summit stump. Caleb reads it as "moving house." The prose never says the ants
  know a storm is coming; the reader may.
- **Hallway photographs are planted** (one sentence in the van) so that Chapter 2's
  discovery of Daniel in a station group photo has somewhere to land.
- **Marco drove out; Ines drove home.** Small, deliberate: it is how the "back of
  her head" closing line works.
- **Rachel is off-page.** She receives the mud photo. Her first on-page beat stays
  in Chapter 2.
- **Ines's clock** was corrected across the draft and the mini-arc outline to a
  single rule (off by half past twelve, back by one); the call to leave is at
  eleven, the saddle is crossed at 11:22–11:24 with the water at shin depth.
