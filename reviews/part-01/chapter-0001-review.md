## What the chapter does

Three scenes, one field day, six hours, joined by spoken transitions rather than breaks. Scene one: the boathouse at six, the
luggage-scale argument, the van, the bar coming up out of the water, Ines's rule
(off the bar by half past twelve, back at the station by one), and Caleb in the
lee-side mud at half past seven. Scene two: the island. Ines splits the team;
Nora goes to the shore logger with Ines, Caleb counts birds and sweeps for

twins and go to Ines, who verifies against Gus's station reading and pulls the team
ninety minutes early, leaving the battery swap, the count and the download for a
return trip. The saddle crossing with the pole. Scene three: the van, the scale,
Caleb losing to a wet glove, and Rachel's texts.

## What changed from the card and why

- **Crabs are omitted.** The card has "crabs retreating" among the creature
  signals. The world-creature specialist's advice was that on a northern gravel
  bar in October the shore crabs are under stones and weed by day and would not be
  visible retreating; a listener would picture something the beach would not show.
  The signal is carried by the shorebirds lifting together and the ants instead,
  which the card also names. Flagging rather than adding a crab beat that the
  biology would not support.
- **The spider web beat is not spent here.** Chapter 3's card owns the web on the
  dashboard. Nothing here pre-empts it.
- **The ants react to a wet log, not to the weather.** Carpenter ants move brood
  when the nest is compromised; the drift log at the wrack line is soaked from
  underneath (the water at low was already high), so they relocate to the dry
  summit stump. Caleb reads it as "moving house." The prose never says the ants
  know a storm is coming; the reader may.
- **Hallway photographs are planted** (one sentence in the van) so that Chapter 2's
  discovery of Daniel in a station group photo has somewhere to land.
- **Marco drove out; Ines drove home.** Small, deliberate: it is how the "back of
  her head" closing line works.
- **Rachel is off-page.** She receives the mud photo. Her first on-page beat stays
  in Chapter 2.
- **Ines's clock** was corrected across the draft and the mini-arc outline to a
  single rule (off by half past twelve, back by one); the call to leave is at
  eleven, the saddle is crossed at 11:22–11:24 with the water at shin depth.

## Specialist findings: acted on

- Scene: cut from six sections to three scenes; narrator sentences that made
  Caleb sound older than eighteen removed; the summit box is a picture Caleb
  walks past, not an object he examines.
- Character: no twin telepathy, no "twin thing", no shared count. The halves of the
  warning are two different kinds of noticing that happen to agree, which is the
  Chapter 5 engine in miniature. Nora wins the order of speaking because she has
  the laptop; Caleb minds.
- World-creature: ants black with rust-coloured legs, brood pale and rice-sized,
  larger larvae carried out in front, a worker carried curled beneath another
  (adult transport, which is real); the trail is an existing route; crabs omitted
  (above); gull takes a sandwich by landing and considering, not by snatching.
- Mystery: the box is "clean and dry ... the only thing up there that wasn't
  rusting" and nothing more. No hand, no tag, no wire, no name. It is not in
  `mysteries.introduced` because Caleb registers nothing; it enters the ladder at
  Chapter 7 (M34).
- Continuity: "Download stays" added so the return trip has a real reason;
  the padlock is unspecified so that Chapter 9's lock code (a combination) does not
  contradict it; "shore logger" kept as a phrase distinct from "enclosure".

## Downstream constraints recorded by continuity

- Chapter 1 is a Tuesday in the first week of October, low water about 10:30.
  Chapter 3's Saturday lows then fall at roughly 13:50 and 02:15, so a morning
  skiff run is valid and a foot crossing is not.
- Chapter 10's Thursday must be +9 or +23 days from this Tuesday for the tide to
  put a low in working hours, not +16.
- The summit padlock must be a combination padlock when Chapter 9 gives its code.
- Left on the island for the return trip: the spare battery, the unfinished count,
  the unfinished download, and one transect stake lost at the saddle.
