## Chapter 1 — The Alarm

Jack discovers the unauthorized access. TOMBS begins behaving outside his control.
The situation escalates.

Ends on his credentials revoked and "I didn't."

## Chapter 2 — The Boundary

Jack and Sarah attempt to stop TOMBS. They discover that the developed settlement has
become the target. The array continues toward activation.

Ends on "They're running it."

## Chapter 3 — The Activation

TOMBS activates. The developed settlement is reduced in scale. Jack and Sarah
investigate what happened and discover that the town has shrunk while the rest of the
island remains normal size.

Ends on "Their town had shrunk. And the island had not."

## How movements work

We write about three connected chapters at a time, find the natural endings and
cliffhangers inside them, test the result as audio through ElevenLabs, revise anything
confusing to the ear, and only then move on. There is no outline running fifty
chapters ahead, and one is not to be invented. See `outline/WORKFLOW.md`.
