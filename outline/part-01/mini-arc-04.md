# Mini-Arc 4 — Building the Impossible (Chapters 31–40)

**Purpose:** a working device, real danger, real confirmation, and someone trying
directly to stop or warn them. First appearance of future recurring allies (Wes
Tanaka, properly; Kessler's arc turns).
**Outside clock:** March to late August, Year 1. Engineering takes months; the
chapters use explicit time-skips.
**Answered:** the device works on living tissue at small scale; Dad very likely
survived, at least initially.
**Opened:** why would anyone warn them off something that supposedly doesn't exist;
what the organization actually wants; why the island can't be found (M03).
**Emotional arc:** resolve hardens into full commitment; Rachel stops trying to prevent
it and instead makes herself useful.
**Hook out:** a cryptic warning that implies whoever gave it already knows more than
the twins do.

Caleb carries six of these ten chapters; the building is his.

### Chapter 0031 — Inventory
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Police take a report and lose interest. Rachel's fury turns into commitment: if they are going to be robbed for it, they will finish it. Inventory of what survived: the rig, both notebooks, the coordinates (Nora photographed the torn page the day she found it). Caleb's cast comes off; his wrist is weak for months and the outline tracks it.
- **Character beat:** Caleb and Rachel, allied, are more alike than either likes.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** none advanced; commitment made.
- **Ending type:** decision
- **Hands off:** the region.

### Chapter 0032 — The Region
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Nora and Kessler model the region from the coordinates, the tide table, and the Tanaka logs: a patch of open Pacific where instruments drift, fishing crews turn back, and every satellite tile is "cloud" in every season. Two layers become visible: something distorts readings near the boundary, and something else tidies up the readings that get through. Introduced: why can't the island be found.
- **Character beat:** Nora is happiest inside this problem and Caleb, watching, understands for the first time that her stillness is not indecision.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** +M03 (why can't the island be found: two layers, both hinted). M02 (advanced).
- **Ending type:** discovery
- **Hands off:** the coil.

### Chapter 0033 — Coil
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The real device: Caleb and Rachel wind the coil over a week; power draw the house can't take; a borrowed generator from Harlow's. The near-miss: during a test the field catches Caleb's right hand. For a second his hand is elsewhere. It comes back. It is numb for nine days and never quite trusts the cold again (status change, permanent, minor).
- **Character beat:** Nora's fear finally shows as anger; Caleb's answer is to keep his hands in his pockets for a chapter.
- **Creature/world:** Moths at the garage window every test night, in numbers that are wrong for March.
- **Mysteries:** M17 (background: living things respond to the field).
- **Ending type:** threat
- **Hands off:** the buoy archive.

### Chapter 0034 — Years After
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** In an oceanographic buoy archive Nora finds an event cluster in the region dated seven years after Daniel vanished, with a signature that matches their bench readings line for line. Something ran a device like theirs, out there, years after he was supposedly dead. The strongest evidence yet that he survived, at least initially.
- **Character beat:** Caleb needs it to be him. Nora needs it to be verified. Rachel, told, sits down on the stairs and doesn't get up for a while.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** +M01 (did Dad survive, reach the island, did people there know him; formally raised). M02 (advanced).
- **Ending type:** revelation
- **Hands off:** Wes.

### Chapter 0035 — Wes
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Caleb asks Wes Tanaka to take them out to the region. Wes, who has heard the dead-zone stories his whole life and half believes them, wants money, a straight answer, and to see it. He gets two of the three. Ally established: Wes will matter later in Rachel's own thread.
- **Character beat:** Caleb tells the truth because he cannot help it; it is the right call for once and Nora says so, which costs her.
- **Creature/world:** The Tanaka boat's bilge has its own ecosystem; Wes narrates it.
- **Mysteries:** M03 (advanced: the Tanaka family has turned back from that water for three generations).
- **Ending type:** funny character beat
- **Hands off:** Halloran, again.

### Chapter 0036 — Pressure
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The organization tries directly. Halloran appears at the house with an offer to buy "Daniel's materials" and a kindness about Rachel's health that is not kind. The same week Kessler's department receives an inquiry about his old institute affiliation and he steps back, ashamed, and says so plainly. The twins lose their expert.
- **Character beat:** Caleb takes Kessler's withdrawal as betrayal; Nora understands it. Their positions flip from Chapter 19, and they notice.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M07 (advanced: the organization's interest is active). M22 (background: Kessler's parting line, that he thinks the phenomenon is older than Daniel). M30 (advanced: Kessler's fear is specific).
- **Ending type:** betrayal
- **Hands off:** full scale.

### Chapter 0037 — Full Scale
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The device is finished. The first full-scale test on a living thing: a potted plant, then, because Caleb will not be argued out of it, a mouse in a ventilated box. Both shift and return whole after seconds. It works. The cost is in the numbers: to shift a person it needs power the garage cannot make. The notebook's answer is the region itself: the anomaly's own instability is the missing power. Rule 3, arrived at from the inside.
- **Character beat:** Nora runs the numbers three times and then says the sentence she has been avoiding for a month: they will have to go.
- **Creature/world:** The mouse is fine. Nora keeps it.
- **Mysteries:** M02 (advanced). The "why warn us off something that doesn't exist" question is now inescapable.
- **Ending type:** discovery
- **Hands off:** what it costs.

### Chapter 0038 — What It Costs
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Rachel stops trying to prevent it. She breaks the promise on her own terms: she tells them about the interviewer eighteen years ago, someone who was not police and would not say who they represented, and gives them Daniel's last letter to her from before the trip. She does not mention the signal. She sells the thing that pays for the boat.
- **Character beat:** The family, as a unit, chooses. Caleb finally sees what the eighteen years cost and says so, badly, and it lands anyway.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (advanced: the interviewer; the signal still withheld). M07 (advanced).
- **Ending type:** emotional beat
- **Hands off:** provisioning.

### Chapter 0039 — Provisioning
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Preparation at two scales: what they can carry now, what will still be useful at seven millimetres (thread, needles, a blade, salt, the device's core). Nora's blind spot bites: she delays departure a week to verify one reading, and in that week the marina revokes Wes's slip on a paperwork "irregularity." Someone pays for her hesitation; they will have to leave from a different harbour, fast.
- **Character beat:** Nora owns it out loud. Caleb does not say "I told you," which is its own growth.
- **Creature/world:** On the last test night a moth lands on the humming device and, for a second, is the wrong size.
- **Mysteries:** M17 (background, sharpened: the field does something to insects). M06 (advanced: pressure through paperwork).
- **Ending type:** creature encounter
- **Hands off:** the dock.

### Chapter 0040 — Cast Off
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Departure at night from a borrowed berth. Rachel on the dock, staying: "someone has to be here when it comes back," a line the twins take as fear and the audience may take as more. As the lines go, a message reaches Caleb's phone from Halloran's number, the warning that closes the arc: they will find what they're looking for, and that is not the good news.
- **Character beat:** Caleb and Nora, two-count, on the deck. Rachel does not wave; she watches until they're gone.
- **Creature/world:** Harbour lights, moths, the last of the normal world.
- **Mysteries:** the warning implies knowledge: why warn them off something that supposedly doesn't exist, and what does the organization want (M07, M06 sharpened toward Part 6). M13 (Rachel's line).
- **Ending type:** quiet unsettling line
- **Hands off:** open water.
