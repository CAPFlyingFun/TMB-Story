# Mini-Arc 1 — Ordinary Gravity (Chapters 1–10)

**Purpose:** establish the siblings' real world before anything breaks it. One
competence scene each, a guarded Rachel, a crack in the family story, the drive, the
crash, the split vision.
**Outside clock:** early to mid October, Year 0. Caleb and Nora are 18, a few months
past their birthday.
**Answered:** nothing, by design.
**Opened:** why their visions don't match (M05); why part of what they saw doesn't feel
invented (M04); Rachel's guardedness as subtext (M13).
**Emotional arc:** ordinary → shaken → the first vulnerable conversation between the
twins in years.
**Hook out:** comparing notes in the hospital, they realize the two visions fit like
halves of one image.

Provisional setting used throughout (swap freely, see `overview.md`): the Bennetts live
in Port Alder, a mid-sized city on the Pacific Northwest coast. Caleb works at Harlow's
Boatyard. Nora works part-time in the digitization room of Cascade State University's
library and is auditing an engineering course there. Rachel is a calibration engineer
at Tidewater Instruments, a marine-instrument firm.

### Chapter 0001 — The Wet Engine
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** A customer brings a drowned outboard to Harlow's and a story that doesn't fit the damage. Caleb finds the real fault by feel (water in the wrong chamber for the story told) and, reading the man, gets the truth out of him without accusing him. First competence scene: hands and people.
- **Character beat:** Home for dinner. The twins' banter, the "two-count" ritual (they count to two before doing anything stupid together; childhood habit), Dad as a framed photograph nobody looks at directly. A customer's "you two must have that twin thing" gets the dry, practiced answer.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world. A wasp nest under the boatyard eaves Caleb keeps meaning to deal with; he watches them instead. Insects as texture from page one.
- **Mysteries:** none formally. Dad's absence stated as the family tells it: research trip, boat lost at sea, body never recovered, eighteen years ago.
- **Ending type:** funny character beat
- **Hands off:** Nora's turn; a letter on the counter Rachel folded away too fast.

### Chapter 0002 — Pattern of Absences
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** In the digitization room Nora is handed a stack of "independent" public complaints for scanning and notices two of them use the same unusual phrase. She proves they share an author from metadata alone. Second competence scene: structure and pattern. Her supervisor is impressed and slightly unnerved.
- **Character beat:** Dinner. Nora watches Rachel force a smile over the folded letter and files it. Caleb doesn't notice; Nora doesn't say. Establish her protectiveness toward Mom reading, to Caleb, as coldness.
- **Creature/world:** The library's old wing has an ant trail along the skirting that the building staff have given up on. Nora has mapped it in her head.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (subtext): the letter.
- **Ending type:** quiet unsettling line
- **Hands off:** Rachel announces a garage clear-out this weekend.

### Chapter 0003 — Boxes
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The garage clear-out. Dad's boxes come down for the first time in years. Caleb wants to read everything; Nora wants to catalogue it. Among tide tables for the local coast is one for a region of the Pacific thousands of miles from where the boat was supposedly lost, marked in Dad's hand. Rachel takes that box back a little too quickly.
- **Character beat:** Caleb's hunger for the best version of his father, shown not stated: he pockets a photograph. The guarded Rachel scene begins here and continues in Chapter 4.
- **Creature/world:** Silverfish in the box lids; a spider egg sac in a corner Caleb leaves alone.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (subtext); the tide table is the first geographic seed of M02 without anyone naming it.
- **Ending type:** piece of evidence
- **Hands off:** Nora, alone with Rachel.

### Chapter 0004 — What Mom Doesn't Say
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Nora visits Rachel at Tidewater Instruments and watches her calibrate a deep-water pressure sensor: the mother's competence, shown. Then the question about the tide table. Rachel's deflection is fluent, practiced, and Nora recognizes practice when she hears it.
- **Character beat:** That night the twins argue for the first time on the page about Dad: Caleb needs a father worth missing; Nora needs her mother not to be hurt again. Neither is wrong. Rev 1 §3's disagreement, surfacing early as instructed.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (subtext hardens: Rachel has a script).
- **Ending type:** emotional beat
- **Hands off:** Caleb, who decides they will ask her properly.

### Chapter 0005 — Two-Count
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** An ordinary week with weight: a night shift at the boatyard, a fishing crew's story about a "dead zone" out past the shelf where instruments drift (told as sailor talk; plant only). The twins on the roof, the ritual, the inside joke about Dad.
- **Character beat:** Caleb acts sooner than is wise: he tells Nora he has already asked Rachel to sit down with them Friday. Nora is furious he moved without her and then agrees. First real vulnerable conversation, attempted and only half-managed.
- **Creature/world:** Moths at the roof light. The dead-zone story is the earliest breath of M03 and of Wes Tanaka's family lore (Chapter 24).
- **Mysteries:** none advanced.
- **Ending type:** decision
- **Hands off:** Friday.

### Chapter 0006 — The Question
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 1
- **Event:** The sit-down. Rachel gives a fuller story than ever before: Daniel's lab did storage logistics, a research trip, the boat lost, the lab closed afterward. It is more than they have ever had and it is still curated. Nora, who digitized the university's old bulletins last spring, knows the lab's closure was announced before the trip, not after. She does not say so.
- **Character beat:** Caleb takes the story as a gift. Nora takes it as a second inconsistency. She keeps it from him, and knows she is doing to him what Rachel does to them.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (advanced in subtext).
- **Ending type:** unanswered question
- **Hands off:** A rainy night drive home from Rachel's work event the next week.

### Chapter 0007 — Rain on the Coast Road
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The drive home. In the car the conversation the roof didn't manage: Caleb admits he imagines Dad most days; Nora admits she's afraid for Mom more than she misses a man she never met. Then the crash, a genuine accident: a truck hydroplanes across the centre line on the coast road. Violence, brief, exact, audio-clear.
- **Character beat:** In the impact, Caleb's fragment of the vision: a man's hoarse voice shouting to others, "Get them under. Hold the line," a sound like rain on a drum or a hive, hands gripping a cable, the smell of wet soil. No face. No name.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M04 and M05 begin here without ids yet: the vision is real (locked) and split by focus (locked).
- **Ending type:** revelation
- **Hands off:** the same seconds, from Nora's side.

### Chapter 0008 — Halves
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The crash from Nora's seat, overlapping Chapter 7 by seconds, then the aftermath: trapped, rain through the broken window, Caleb bleeding and talking, her phone, first responders. Her fragment: a vaulted space of roots, a lattice of small lights in a pattern she could redraw, a wall scored with nested brackets, a doorway that is not a door. No voice.
- **Character beat:** Nora's mind does what it does: she counts the lights. Her hesitation blind spot is seeded as a strength here (she stays still, correctly).
- **Creature/world:** Normal world, but the vision's space is unmistakably underground and alive.
- **Mysteries:** M04, M05 (see Chapter 10 for formal introduction).
- **Ending type:** arrival
- **Hands off:** Mercy General, first night.

### Chapter 0009 — Concussion Protocol
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Hospital, first night. Injuries recorded: Caleb, fractured left wrist (cast for six weeks) and cuts; Nora, concussion and a cracked rib. Rachel arrives and is, for once, not composed. Caleb tells the doctor nothing about the voice. He tells Nora in a whisper. She goes very quiet.
- **Character beat:** Caleb sees Rachel clearly for the first time in the story: what eighteen years cost her, in the way she holds Nora's chart.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M04, M05.
- **Ending type:** quiet unsettling line (Nora: "There was a line. I saw it.")
- **Hands off:** the room, next morning.

### Chapter 0010 — Fit
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 1
- **Event:** The comparison, done properly. Caleb's voice fits inside Nora's space; the rhythm of his hive-sound matches the pattern of her lights; "get them under" matches her doorway that is not a door. Two fragments, one moment. They agree not to tell Rachel yet, and both know that is a bad sign about the family.
- **Character beat:** The first vulnerable conversation, completed: Nora tells Caleb the closure-date inconsistency she withheld. He forgives her faster than she deserves and that becomes a pattern too.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** +M04 (was the vision real), +M05 (why did it split), formally introduced.
- **Ending type:** discovery
- **Hands off:** Discharge, and a neurologist with an explanation.
