# Mini-Arc 3 — The Weight of the Journal (Chapters 21–30)

> **Note (2026-09-16, decision 0008):** this file predates the Trello plan. The
> ten-chapter overview cards on the TRADDOMIUM boards are the current plan for
> these chapters; where they differ, Trello wins and this file is reconciled
> before any of these chapters is drafted. Known ripples: Caleb's cast becomes a
> brace after six weeks ("cast" below reads "brace" from mid-November); Ch. 28's
> boatyard no longer exists (Halloran approaches Caleb at Alder Sound instead);
> Ch. 18's "one detail" already landed in Ch. 10; Nora has no library job.

**Purpose:** the real research surfaces; stakes become physical. Kessler becomes a
cautious ally; Rachel moves from obstacle to reluctant ally; the break-in.
**Outside clock:** December, Year 0, to February, Year 1.
**Answered:** Dad really was researching scale manipulation, and the family's official
story was a deliberate cover.
**Opened:** who funded him (M07); why the project was erased (M06 deepens); what the
break-in was actually looking for; the organization now has a face (Halloran).
**Emotional arc:** Rachel shifts from obstacle to reluctant ally; the twins' shared
purpose starts to outweigh their disagreement.
**Hook out:** the thing taken in the break-in was something Rachel had specifically
kept hidden, and it's gone.

### Chapter 0021 — Reconstruction
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Kessler, told everything, agrees to help on conditions (never at his office, never by phone). From the journal they reconstruct the shape of Daniel's work: the fundable cover, spatial-compression storage and transport; the side theory, that the effect resonated with something already present in specific ocean regions where the lab's instruments kept reading anomalies. He explains rule 2 in a teacher's terms: not shrinking, a shift of state.
- **Character beat:** Nora finally has a structure to map and is, for the first time since the crash, calm. Kessler notices she is the one who asks what's missing.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world; Kessler's office plant has aphids he keeps because "someone should study what thrives on neglect."
- **Mysteries:** M30 (advanced). M22 (background: Kessler's phrase "already present").
- **Ending type:** discovery
- **Hands off:** the garage.

### Chapter 0022 — Bench Test
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** From a sketch in the journal, Caleb builds a crude replica of a resonance test rig on the garage bench, cast and all. Rachel walks in mid-wiring and, instead of stopping him, tells him the ground is on the wrong side. The first experiment fails: nothing shifts. A compass on the shelf swings and does not swing back.
- **Character beat:** Rachel's dry competence is the warmest thing in the chapter; Caleb sees her, properly, for the second time.
- **Creature/world:** A house spider in the rafters relocates during the test and does not return. Nobody comments.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (advanced: Rachel can read the sketch).
- **Ending type:** funny character beat
- **Hands off:** Kessler talks.

### Chapter 0023 — Kessler's Confession
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 1
- **Event:** In a diner off campus, Kessler tells them what he has kept his head down about for eighteen years: Daniel was drawn into private, defence-adjacent money; the "accident" story arrived pre-written; he suspected and did nothing. He gives them the funder's name, Corbel Applied Research (provisional), and one more thing: the after-hours access logs had a second name he never learned. Daniel was not working alone.
- **Character beat:** Kessler's guilt is real and not the twins' to absolve; Nora doesn't try. Caleb does, and Kessler declines it gently.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** +M07 (who funded/monitored; see the flag in `overview.md`). M30 (advanced: his connection is professional and guilty).
- **Ending type:** piece of evidence
- **Hands off:** the marina.

### Chapter 0024 — The Second Name
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Caleb takes the physical trail: the moorage Daniel's boat launched from. The old harbourmaster, Tanaka, remembers the boat and remembers two people aboard. His grandson Wes (provisional), who runs the family boat now, digs out the fuel log: a second signature, illegible. First appearance of Wes Tanaka, and of the Tanaka family's dead-zone stories told straight rather than as sailor talk.
- **Character beat:** Caleb and Wes are the same kind of person and take to each other in a page, which Nora will later call "your usual due diligence."
- **Creature/world:** Barnacles, gulls, the marina's under-dock life; Wes knows every crab hole.
- **Mysteries:** M07 (advanced: a second person). M03 (background: the dead zone).
- **Ending type:** unanswered question
- **Hands off:** Rachel, at the crawlspace hatch.

### Chapter 0025 — What Mom Kept
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Rachel brings up what she kept from the crawlspace: prototype parts, a second notebook in Daniel's hand, and the promise. He made her swear that if anything happened she would not go looking. She has kept it for eighteen years and it has cost her both her children's trust. She does not mention a signal or an interviewer; those stay hers.
- **Character beat:** Nora understands the promise instantly and hates that she does. Caleb is angrier than he expected to be, and it is the first time his anger is at Rachel rather than for her.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (advanced: the promise and the materials; the signal and the interviewer remain hidden).
- **Ending type:** emotional beat
- **Hands off:** the second notebook has a working circuit.

### Chapter 0026 — Second Attempt
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** With the real notebook the rig is rebuilt in a night. Second experiment: a glass marble on a scale loses its weight for four tenths of a second and reappears three centimetres to the left, warm. The coil browns the bench. It works, barely, and it is plainly dangerous. Rachel, Nora and Caleb sit on the garage floor and nobody speaks.
- **Character beat:** Caleb's hands did this; his need to act is, for once, exactly right.
- **Creature/world:** Every insect in the garage has gone still or gone. Caleb notices the silence before the result.
- **Mysteries:** M02 (advanced: the effect is real, so the region matters). M23 (background only, not introduced: shift, not shrink).
- **Ending type:** discovery
- **Hands off:** Nora assembles the timeline.

### Chapter 0027 — Cover Story
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Nora lays it all on the wall: the lab closed before the trip; the memorial was arranged through the funder's lawyer; the family's story was written by someone else. Rachel confirms it. She was told what to say and by whom she will not yet say, only "they sent someone." Answered: the official story was a deliberate cover.
- **Character beat:** Nora and Rachel, alone, for the first time as colleagues rather than mother and daughter.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M06 (advanced: the erasure began the week he vanished). M13 (advanced: "they sent someone").
- **Ending type:** revelation
- **Hands off:** a visitor at the boatyard.

### Chapter 0028 — Halloran
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** A woman in her fifties, pleasant, unhurried, finds Caleb at Harlow's. She gives the name Halloran (provisional), asks after his mother's health, asks whether the family "still has Daniel's things," and leaves a card with a phone number and no company. First face of the organization.
- **Character beat:** Caleb reads her as he reads everyone and gets nothing, which frightens him more than a threat would have. He does not take the card. He memorizes the number.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M06, M07 (advanced: the organization is present and knows the family). M13 (Rachel, told, goes white: she has met someone like this before; she does not say so yet).
- **Ending type:** quiet unsettling line
- **Hands off:** precautions.

### Chapter 0029 — Precautions
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** They move the materials, and argue about where. Kessler, by a borrowed phone, tells them to stop calling him from the house. Nora makes a plan; Caleb executes it; Rachel improves it. The three of them working as one unit for the first time.
- **Character beat:** The shared purpose outweighs the disagreement, visibly: Nora accepts acting on incomplete information; Caleb accepts waiting a day to do it right.
- **Creature/world:** The garage spider is back, in the rig's frame. Rachel says leave it.
- **Mysteries:** none advanced; the board is set.
- **Ending type:** decision
- **Hands off:** the house, empty for an afternoon.

### Chapter 0030 — Break-In
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** They come home from Rachel's work to a house that has been searched by professionals: nothing broken, everything touched. The rig is untouched, the notebooks are untouched. What is gone is the one thing Rachel had hidden separately from everything else: a sealed envelope Daniel left her before the trip, "to be opened only if," which in eighteen years she never opened. Nobody, including the audience, knows what it said.
- **Character beat:** Rachel's grief for the envelope is the first time the twins see her mourn him rather than manage him.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M06 (advanced: they wanted something specific). M13 (advanced and re-opened: what was in the envelope is now a question for the far future).
- **Ending type:** threat
- **Hands off:** "Then we build it properly."
