**Purpose:** the voyage, the shrink, the arrival, the spider, the rescue. Per Rev 2
§10, arrival does not mean walking up to a gate: the twins land in wild terrain,
inexperienced, and are found by people who understand this world completely.
**Outside clock:** early September, Year 1, roughly eleven months after the crash.
**Island clock:** begins at Chapter 44. Tracked separately from here on.
**Answered:** the island is real; the technology works on living people; an organized
human settlement exists there.
**Opened:** the island's scale and geography don't match the calculations; who are these
two, from the islanders' side (M29); the shield, named in passing (M11 seed).
**Emotional arc:** eleven months of obsession made suddenly, overwhelmingly real.
**Hook out:** the rescue's aftermath. Adrenaline, a spear still half-raised, and the
only question that matters: *"Where did you come from?"*

Caleb carries six of these ten chapters; the voyage and the crossing are physical.
Maya appears here in a smaller capacity, as the patrol's overwatch, with no flying
creature on the page: her signature species is provisional (Rev 3 §5) and is not
committed in Part One.

### Chapter 0041 — Open Water
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The voyage begins. Wes, the boat, the crated device, Nora seasick and furious about it. Three days out the first anomaly: compass and GPS begin to disagree with each other and with the stars. Wes has seen it before and turned back before. He doesn't.
- **Character beat:** Wes asks the twin-telepathy question and gets the answer; the three of them become a crew in the laugh that follows.
- **Creature/world:** Flying fish, a following gull, the ocean as a place with its own weather of living things.
- **Mysteries:** M03 (advanced: the boundary distorts instruments, layer one confirmed by experience).
- **Ending type:** funny character beat
- **Hands off:** clocks.

### Chapter 0042 — Clocks
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The second anomaly: after one night the boat's clocks disagree with the shore radio's time signal by forty minutes, and the disagreement grows. Nora logs it hourly and will not speculate aloud. She is the better one for this and Caleb admits it.
- **Character beat:** Nora, calm inside the unknown, is the one who keeps Wes from turning back.
- **Creature/world:** The sea goes glassy and the following gull leaves.
- **Mysteries:** M12 (background seed only; the ladder introduces time distortion in Part 4, and this chapter plants without naming). M03 (advanced).
- **Ending type:** unanswered question
- **Hands off:** the calm.

### Chapter 0043 — The Calm
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The third anomaly: a dead calm where the surface of the sea is subtly wrong, as if the horizon were nearer than it should be. A dragonfly lands on the rail, the size of a gull. Caleb touches it. It is real, warm, and it lifts off. Things here do not hold one scale.
- **Character beat:** Caleb's hands, again: he cannot not touch it. Nora photographs it and the photograph is blurred in a way the camera can't explain.
- **Creature/world:** The first creature of the island's world, met at the boundary, at the wrong size. Written for wonder, not horror.
- **Mysteries:** M17 (seeded on the page: a shifted insect at partial state). M22 (background).
- **Ending type:** creature encounter
- **Hands off:** the readings spike.

### Chapter 0044 — Threshold
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The instability the notebook predicted arrives and will not last. Nora wants one more hour of readings; Caleb says now. Two-count. Wes holds the wheel and stays with the boat. The device is powered from the anomaly's own instability, exactly as Chapter 37 predicted. The shrink, from the inside: the world's sound goes up an octave, the rail becomes a cliff, the sea a moving mountain, and then, because two points distant in one state can be adjacent in another, they are not on the boat at all.
- **Character beat:** Caleb acts; Nora lets him; they do it together. The pattern, finally working for them.
- **Creature/world:** The crossing is written as the single most sensory sequence of Part One. Full weight, no checklist.
- **Mysteries:** M02 (answered in the body: the technology works on living people). M03 (rule 3 experienced).
- **Ending type:** arrival
- **Hands off:** grass.

### Chapter 0045 — Grass
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Nora wakes on soil under grass that is architecture. Inventory at scale: Nora's ankle is wrenched; Caleb's right palm is burned from the device's core; the device is with them and dead, its power spent. Water beads like glass boulders. Sound is enormous. They are on land, which means the island is real.
- **Character beat:** Nora maps: she finds north by the sun and is wrong, and does not yet know why. Caleb finds water first because he listens.
- **Creature/world:** First sight of the insect-scale world from the structural mind: grass as columns, a leaf as a roof, a beetle track as a road.
- **Mysteries:** M02 (advanced: land; the island is real). Status: Nora ankle, Caleb palm.
- **Ending type:** discovery
- **Hands off:** water from the sky.

### Chapter 0046 — Water
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Survival logic, first lesson: a rain shower at seven millimetres is artillery. They shelter under a leaf edge, then under a root when the leaf fails. Hunger. An ant scout crosses their shelter, pauses, tastes the air, and ignores them: the first showcased creature, doing exactly what an ant does. Caleb cuts a thorn into a spear.
- **Character beat:** Caleb is useful with his hands for the first time since the burn and needs it.
- **Creature/world:** Rain as the world's first predator. The ant, indifferent, is scarier than the dragonfly was.
- **Mysteries:** none advanced; survival.
- **Ending type:** threat
- **Hands off:** a cut path.

### Chapter 0047 — The Line
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Nora sees structure: the grass ahead has been cut in a corridor, and there is a post, waist-high to them, notched and weathered, made by hands. People. And the geography is wrong: the ridge she saw from the boat should be east; by the sun it is not, and the sun itself moved faster than her count. The island does not match the calculations; the anomaly is more complex than they modelled. Then the insects go quiet.
- **Character beat:** Nora's certainty-need, turned outward, is what saves them here: she notices the silence and stops Caleb walking into it.
- **Creature/world:** The managed corridor is the first sign of the settlement's reach. The silence is the wolf spider's.
- **Mysteries:** M02 (answered: an organized human presence exists). M03, M12 (background: the geography and the sun). M17 (background).
- **Ending type:** quiet unsettling line
- **Hands off:** stalked.

### Chapter 0048 — Stalked
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The wolf spider. Not gore: patience. It waits at the corridor's edge, larger than they are, eyes catching light, and when they move it follows at a distance that never changes. Caleb reads it the way he reads people and understands it is not hunting yet; it is learning them. Night falls with it still there.
- **Character beat:** Caleb cannot tolerate not acting; Nora holds him still by the wrist he broke and they last the night.
- **Creature/world:** Written from the bible's wolf-spider entry as it will be approved: a hunter that runs down prey, uses cover, and is patient. Its behaviour is normal for a spider and wrong for its location, which only the islanders will recognize.
- **Mysteries:** M17 (advanced: this one is at the twins' scale and lethal). M29 (seeded: something in known territory that shouldn't be there).
- **Ending type:** decision (run for the post at first light)
- **Hands off:** wrong prey.

### Chapter 0049 — Wrong Prey
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** First light. They run. The spider commits. Then the ground shivers in a rhythm Nora recognizes as deliberate: a column of ants cuts between the twins and the spider, laying vibration across the corridor; the spider, confused by prey it can feel but not find, turns. A beetle's shell comes through the grass like a moving wall with a person on it. The rescue, seen by someone who does not understand any of what she is seeing and can only see that it is competent.
- **Character beat:** Nora's whole mind reaches for the pattern and fails, and she lets it, and looks.
- **Creature/world:** Trained ants for vibration and distraction; a beetle for cover and extraction. The islanders' craft shown as knowledge and positioning, never brute force (Rev 2 §10).
- **Mysteries:** M01 (background: people live here; they may have known him). M29 (advanced).
- **Ending type:** arrival
- **Hands off:** the aftermath.

### Chapter 0050 — Where Did You Come From
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The rescue completes. Theo Calder directs the ant line from the beetle's back with a handful of signals; Finn Okoro on foot draws the spider's attention with more nerve than sense; Maya Voss on the rise above with a bow, calling ranges. What finally makes the wild spider leave is another of its kind wearing a harness, brought up by a handler nobody introduces. Then the adrenaline that hasn't faded, Finn's spear still half-raised, Theo studying one face and then the other, and the only question that matters: *"Where did you come from?"* Part One closes on the question.
- **Character beat:** Caleb, who reads people, reads three strangers who understand this world completely and thinks: how are they doing that. He does not answer the question. Nora, behind him, is already counting the ants.
- **Creature/world:** The full showcased set on one page: ants, beetle, wild wolf spider, tamed wolf spider. Theo's line about the twins being "this deep inside the line" names the shield without explaining it.
- **Mysteries:** +M29 (who are Caleb and Nora, from the islanders' side). M11 (seeded: "the line"). M01 (advanced: this is the place). M02 (answered in full: real, inhabited, organized).
- **Ending type:** unanswered question
- **Hands off:** Part Two, "The Horror": questioning, quarantine, the device changing the room's tone once it is understood.
