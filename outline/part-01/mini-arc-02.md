# Mini-Arc 2 — What the Records Don't Say (Chapters 11–20)

**Purpose:** hospital and early investigation; the first sign someone else is
watching. Professor Kessler's first appearance, recognizing the work, not the face.
**Outside clock:** late October to late November, Year 0.
**Answered:** the vision contained one detail neither twin could have invented (M04
advanced with a confirmed real element).
**Opened:** someone is actively deleting evidence (M06); an organization's fingerprint
surfaces once and vanishes; what is Kessler's real connection to Dad's research (M30).
**Emotional arc:** fear curdling into obsession; the first real disagreement about how
far to take this.
**Hook out:** Dad's sealed file was accessed the same week they started looking.

Nora carries six of these ten chapters; the record-work is hers.

### Chapter 0011 — Discharge
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The neurologist explains a shared post-traumatic confabulation, kindly and completely. Rachel pushes back harder than either twin expects and asks one strangely specific question: did they both report a sound. Then she covers. Home. The twins divide the work without quite saying they are investigating: Nora takes the paper trail, Caleb takes the boxes.
- **Character beat:** Nora files Rachel's question next to the folded letter and the closure date. Three things.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (Rachel's question). M04, M05 held open.
- **Ending type:** decision
- **Hands off:** Caleb, one-handed, in the garage.

### Chapter 0012 — The Journal
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Caleb, wrist in a cast, goes back through Dad's boxes properly. Under a false bottom that isn't hidden so much as forgotten: a field journal. A page torn in half. In the margin of another page, a word in capitals, HALCYON, and a number. Pages of equations in a notation of nested brackets with small dot-subscripts. Rachel finds him with it and, after a long moment, lets him keep it.
- **Character beat:** Caleb's trust instinct: he reads Rachel's letting-him-keep-it as permission. Nora, later, reads it as Rachel choosing which box they were allowed to find.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (Rachel curates). The bracket notation and HALCYON are clues in waiting.
- **Ending type:** piece of evidence
- **Hands off:** Nora at work with the journal photographs.

### Chapter 0013 — Scrubbed
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Nora pulls the institute's old bulletins. Daniel Mercer's lab is in the 2007 print run and absent from the digital archive. The deletion log shows an administrator account provisioned from outside the university, used once, eleven years ago. Someone deleted evidence and did it carefully.
- **Character beat:** Nora is more comfortable inside an unanswered question than Caleb; she does not tell him for two days while she checks, and he is hurt when he learns that.
- **Creature/world:** The skirting-board ants, working; she times them while the query runs.
- **Mysteries:** +M06 (who is erasing evidence of Dad's research).
- **Ending type:** unanswered question
- **Hands off:** Office hours.

### Chapter 0014 — The Bracket
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Nora takes one page of the notation to Professor Adrian Kessler's office hours under a pretext ("an old problem set I can't parse"). He goes still. The nested brackets with dot-subscripts, the boundary term solved first: he has seen this exact hand. He asks where she got it; she lies badly; he lets it go. He tells her the notation belonged to a colleague at the institute, years ago, who "died at sea." First appearance of Kessler.
- **Character beat:** Nora has never lied to a teacher and is bad at it, which Kessler notices and, oddly, seems to respect.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** +M30 (Kessler's real connection to Dad's research; see the flag in `overview.md` on the ladder's Part column).
- **Ending type:** revelation
- **Hands off:** Caleb noticing things at home.

### Chapter 0015 — Someone Was Here
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Small wrong things: the garage padlock re-hung facing the wrong way; a sedan parked twice in a week where nobody parks; a call to Harlow's asking what shifts "the Bennett kid" works. Caleb tells Nora. She says a concussion pattern-matches; he says she'd believe it if it were in a spreadsheet. The first real disagreement about how far to take this, out loud.
- **Character beat:** Caleb acts: he moves the journal to the boatyard. Nora finds out afterward. Trust, both directions, takes a small hit.
- **Creature/world:** The wasp nest under the eaves, finally, someone else's problem: it's been knocked down. Caleb doesn't think it was the wind.
- **Mysteries:** M06 (advanced: surveillance, not just deletion).
- **Ending type:** threat
- **Hands off:** the torn page.

### Chapter 0016 — Coordinates
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The torn page's surviving half carries a latitude and the first digits of a longitude. Cross-referenced with the far-Pacific tide table from Chapter 3, they agree: a region of open ocean roughly two thousand miles from the official accident. Nora maps it. Then: HALCYON, found once in a surviving print index of grant abstracts, attached to a funder's line item with the sponsor redacted. When she goes back the next day, the index volume has been "sent for rebinding."
- **Character beat:** Obsession begins: she starts the wall map that will follow her for the rest of Part One.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** +M02 (is the island real: the region now has a location). M06 (advanced: the index). The organization's fingerprint, once.
- **Ending type:** discovery
- **Hands off:** Caleb asks Rachel for the one thing they have never had.

### Chapter 0017 — Voice
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 1
- **Event:** Caleb asks Rachel whether any recording of Dad's voice exists. There is one: a voicemail from the week before he left, never played for the children. She plays it. Caleb has heard that hoarse cadence once before, in a car, in the rain, saying "hold the line."
- **Character beat:** Rachel watches her son recognize a voice he has never heard and does not ask how. That she doesn't ask is the loudest thing in the chapter.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M04 (advanced: Caleb's fragment matches a real voice). M13 (Rachel does not ask).
- **Ending type:** emotional beat
- **Hands off:** Nora, with the journal and her own memory.

### Chapter 0018 — One Detail
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Nora finds it: in the vision, the brackets scored into the vault wall had a particular quirk, a doubled dot on the inner subscript. The journal, which Caleb found only after the crash and which she had never seen, uses the same doubled dot on every boundary term. One detail neither twin could have invented. The vision contained something real.
- **Character beat:** Fear curdles into obsession. She stops sleeping properly. Caleb notices and does the wrong kind thing (covers for her instead of stopping her).
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M04 (advanced: a confirmed real detail; the ladder's fuller partial answer still waits for Part 3–4). M05 (advanced: her fragment was structural, his was vocal; the split follows the person).
- **Ending type:** piece of evidence
- **Hands off:** the fight.

### Chapter 0019 — How Far
- **POV:** Caleb · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** Caleb wants to go back to Kessler and say everything, then drive to the coast and find whoever knew Dad. Nora wants certainty first. The disagreement is open, loud, and neither wins. Rachel, in the doorway, has heard more than they thought.
- **Character beat:** Caleb underestimates what Rachel knows; Nora, watching Rachel's face, does not. The chapter ends on Rachel's line about the brackets: she taught Daniel to write them that way. Her technical past, revealed in one sentence.
- **Creature/world:** Caleb, in the boatyard bilge at dawn, watches an ant column carry a dead beetle three times its weight and thinks about lifting. Insects stay structural even here.
- **Mysteries:** M13 (advanced: Rachel understands the work).
- **Ending type:** revelation
- **Hands off:** Nora's records request comes back.

### Chapter 0020 — Accessed
- **POV:** Nora · **Scenes:** 2
- **Event:** The university archivist, sympathetic to a good digitizer, tells Nora what a formal request would have hidden: Daniel Mercer's sealed personnel and research file was accessed under an outside authority the same week the twins began looking. Not eleven years ago. This month.
- **Character beat:** Nora tells Caleb the same hour. That, not the news, is the growth.
- **Creature/world:** Normal world.
- **Mysteries:** M06 (advanced: the erasure is current and reactive). M30 (Kessler has not been told; she decides he must be).
- **Ending type:** threat
- **Hands off:** Winter; Kessler, and the real work.
