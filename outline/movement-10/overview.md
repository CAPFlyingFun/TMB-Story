**Event 3, Construction Records.** The engineering archive is a stand-alone server in the records building basement. Its drawings show the man-made island built in modules joined along seams; service galleries under the seams collect rain and drain it to sumps, drawn full. A seam runs near the south side of the settlement, with an access grate just past the end of the southern road; Sarah finds the bars on the camera, the only straight line in the grass. On a clear cold dawn the grate breathes mist: something below is wet. The back room decides: Jack, Mark and Aiden go down in Paul's cage, Unit Four on the winch, Paul's crew at the road end, Sarah running it from the station on the helmet cameras. Stitches out March 13. The crib is finished.

**Event 4, Underground Expedition.** The team pulls Paul's sled through the soil to the grate (a sinking valley crossed on the tarp; the lifeline runs out and is tied off; a ground beetle passes). The gas meter clears the gallery; Jack, Mark and Aiden ride the cage down a shaft as tall as a skyscraper to them. The gallery is vast: tiles like parking lots, a channel wider than a highway, running water. Samples; an unmapped opening in the far wall, marked and forbidden; a centipede brought fast by a dropped jar. Tests: hard water, ordinary bacteria, treatable. Mercer: washing first, drinking after three clean treated tests. Paul's electric pump (no exhaust underground), a generator at the outpost, seventy-three hose lengths to the old irrigation tank. The pump lifts more easily than the shaft's height suggests; nobody knows why. First water March 18. March 19: Unit Four clamps a weeping joint with the beetle coming; the first treated test is clean; 865 L; the drinking tanks now drawn ~630 L/day, about seventeen days left.
