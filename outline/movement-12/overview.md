**Event 7, Ecological Survey.** The gnat lived on the sugary water plants push out of their leaf tips. Sarah designs the Field Book: every road-end camera watched at four fixed times a day, tallied on one sheet, by volunteers who can't do heavy work, from home; watching only. A robin, taller than any building to the town, takes the ground beetle beside the hose line in one strike: open ground is dangerous in daylight; the line walk goes under the stems where it can and under grass-colored tarps where it can't ("It hides us. It doesn't stop it."). After a week the book is a timetable. An ant trail from the eastern road end to the community center's bins is broken by sealing the bins indoors and scrubbing the street with soapy gallery water. Food now runs to about the end of April; the back-hall beans are up. Centipedes in the gallery leave by the far-wall opening (Q13). Sarah 37 weeks, early term.
