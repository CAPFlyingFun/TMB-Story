# Story direction after Chapter 3

**Joshua, 2026-09-18.** This is a DIRECTION, not an outline. It says what the next
stretch is about and what it is not. It does not say how many chapters, and nothing
in it is a licence to draft.

## The headline: we are not rushing to the time jump

> "We are NOT rushing directly into the 18-year time jump after Chapter 3. I want
> several chapters in the immediate aftermath first, letting the miniature-world
> situation become a mystery and giving the settlement time to react, investigate,
> adapt, and survive."

Decision 0018 recorded that the story later jumps about eighteen years and that Jack
and Sarah's son becomes the primary protagonist. **That still stands, and it is not
next.** The aftermath comes first, at its own length.

## What the next stretch explores

Gradually, not all at once, and not as a checklist to tick off:

- emergency response across the roughly five-hundred-person settlement
- what infrastructure survived and what failed
- the loss of outside communications
- attempts to understand or reverse TOMBS
- evidence that the event was deliberate
- the unexplained external vibrations, and the threats of a giant world
- first controlled exploration near the boundary
- Jack and Sarah facing the catastrophe while Sarah is still thirty-two weeks pregnant
- the settlement beginning to realise this may not be reversible quickly
- the early foundations of the society that exists after the eventual jump

Chapter 3 already plants three of these: communications are dead in every direction,
a vibration passes through the building twice with no seismic cause, and Jack decides
he is not certain he wants to know what it was. The aftermath grows from what the
manuscript has already put on the page, not from a plan laid over it.

## What is explicitly NOT decided, and must not be invented

- **The number of pre-jump chapters is open.** Do not fix it.
- **No new fifty-chapter outline.** Decision 0018 retired the last one; the unit of
  work is still a movement of about three chapters, planned with Joshua each time.
- **The son is not the protagonist yet.** He is not born yet.
- **The time jump happens when Joshua deliberately chooses the chapter for it.** Not
  when the aftermath feels long enough, and not because this file exists.
- **Chapters 4 onward are not drafted until he asks.** Recording a direction is not
  the same as being told to write.

## How this relates to the rest

- `architecture/DECISIONS.md` 0018 holds the reboot and the eventual jump.
- `story-rules/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` holds the mysteries the aftermath will pressure —
  who ran TOMBS, how the array drew power it was disconnected from, what the
  vibrations are. Answer them there when a chapter answers one, not here.
- `outline/WORKFLOW.md` still governs how a movement gets made.
