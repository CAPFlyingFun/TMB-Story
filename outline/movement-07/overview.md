## Chapter 21 — The Second List

The station, just after eight on March 8. Doctor Mercer re-dresses Jack's wet stitches (held, a
little pink; she has antibiotics and would rather never open them) and sends Aiden to sleep. Lena
filters and boils the twelve liters; Aiden drinks the first cup ("Flat"). Mark's list of five things
wrong with the trough. Sarah, by speaker: three mornings, not one; on March 6 the spider was
already out at dawn at the warm, lit greenhouse glass. Mark: nobody goes back until they have
watched more mornings from the cameras. Sarah keeps the log ("Watching is fine. Running things is
not." — Mercer). The night impacts are "a different question". Paul's shop at noon: the drop is
wider than a gutter, so the gutter becomes a deep box with a splash board and a lid ("Building it
isn't using it"). Evening: same as high, no protein. Jack talks to the baby, as promised in Ch 20.
Midnight: Aiden reads the old dial thermometer on the greenhouse door frame: eleven, no stars.

## Chapter 22 — No Sun

March 9. Jack builds the crib one-handed while Sarah reads the instructions and watches the
cameras. Low cloud, mild; fewer beads of dew, the tips still drip. Six forty-two passes with no
sun. At six fifty-three the spider comes out and does not sit: it drinks from a drop, passes over a
still gnat, takes one that kicks; a tip drop falls onto the empty road. Flies come to the sugary
tips; one lands on the maintenance cart; Unit Four calls it at once; the spider crosses the asphalt
and takes it beside the cart, where Unit Four stood the day before. Sarah and Aiden work it out: the
sun tells it when, the cold tells it how fast. Cold mornings are the wet ones and the slow ones.
Mark's board: Cold. Clear. Gray. Aiden adds Covered. "Get Paul… I want to see his trough."

## Chapter 23 — Covered

The station yard. Paul's trough (door-wide, knee-deep, three-section lid, splash board; the pole
now has a padded fork and a drip collar). A drum of treated water tipped from the loading platform
carries forward and soaks Mark; the board goes "on our side, between the drop and the people".
The lid sticks on clinging water; rubber strips; close it slow, leave it closed. Aiden's long test:
something living, not much, nothing boiling won't kill — boil it, always. Paul asks whether security
has anything yet on who did it; "Not yet." Mark's numbers (clear sky; eight or under on the door at
first gray; one leaf, one lift, one drop; off at first sun or ten degrees). Mercer: Sarah's pressure
"Better. A little." The crib, half built. Half past ten: stars, seven and falling. Mark: "Then we
test it. First gray. One shake." Paul has already loaded it. Jack sets the alarm for a quarter past
four and runs Sarah through her own rules.
