## Part One normal-world insect texture (OUTLINE, mini-arc-01.md)
- Ch 1: wasp nest under Harlow's Boatyard eaves; Caleb "keeps meaning to deal with; watches instead" (mini-arc-01.md:26). Must still exist until Ch 15, when it is found knocked down and Caleb doubts it was wind (mini-arc-02.md:56). Draft must not have him remove it.
- DRAFT ch0001 wasp facts: PAPER-WASP type comb ("grey comb the size of his open hand, hung on a single stalk", open cells mostly empty; ch0001:47) under the eave of the YARD OFFICE, corner where eave meets wall, above the office door (ch0001:45,79). ~A dozen wasps, sluggish, "October had most of them beaten. The first hard frost would finish the job." (ch0001:47). Present since at least August; a note about it left on the office door in September "in the kind of handwriting that expected to be obeyed" — author unspecified (ch0001:49). One wasp drinks at the wash-bay puddle (ch0001:79). At chapter end all parked on the comb "like a clock somebody had stopped"; Caleb "left them there" (ch0001:109-111). Twins have counted "four times" for the wasps (ch0001:205) — the nest is a running joke, still standing.
- Ch 2: ant trail along the library old wing skirting, mapped in Nora's head.
- Ch 3: silverfish in box lids; spider egg sac Caleb leaves alone.
- Ch 5: moths at the roof light.
- Season: early–mid October, Pacific NW — wasp colonies still active but declining (world-creature-agent's lane to confirm).
