# Creatures

Nothing is established yet. Chapters 1 to 3 contain no creature on the page.

What Chapter 3 establishes is scale, not biology: grass stems taller than buildings, a
tree trunk that will not fit a camera frame, a bead of water that reads as a curved
glass wall, and a deep unexplained vibration that is not a seismic event (see Q06 in
`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

Entries go here as the manuscript introduces them, with the chapter cited. Do not
import anything from `archive/pre-reboot/bible/CREATURES.md`; that bestiary belongs to
the archived story.
