# Timeline

## Fixed points

- **March fifth, in the year twenty-one ten.** The catastrophe `[ch 1]`.
- The night begins at almost eleven o'clock `[ch 1]`. Chapters 1 to 3 are continuous
  and cover that one night and its immediate aftermath.
- Sarah is thirty-two weeks pregnant on that night `[ch 2]`. The baby is a boy.
- "The rest of the world was exactly the same size it had been ten minutes ago" places
  the activation about ten minutes before the closing scene of Chapter 3 `[ch 3]`.
- A full TOMBS test ran the previous day and used less than half the night's power
  draw `[ch 2]`.

## The night, in order

| When | Chapter | Event |
|---|---|---|
| Almost eleven at night | 1 | An unauthorized access alarm wakes Jack. The TOMBS directory opens by itself, the terminal unlocks itself, a remote initialization request cannot be denied, and Jack's administrator credentials are revoked. |
| Continuous | 2 | The array powers up past standby. Lena reports fourteen then fifteen megawatts and disconnects TOMBS from the island grid; it keeps running. The physical shutdown lever stops the rings and they restart. The boundary target is the developed settlement. Jack orders everyone indoors rather than out. |
| Continuous | 3 | TOMBS activates and goes offline because it finished. Jack and Sarah are knocked down and unhurt; the baby moves. External communication is gone. The settlement has been reduced in scale and the island has not. |
