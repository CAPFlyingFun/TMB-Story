## Premise

On March fifth, in the year twenty-one ten, a research settlement on a remote island
in the Atlantic is reduced in scale by its own experimental system. The island does
not change. Only the developed town does. Nearly five hundred people wake to a world
that no longer fits them.

## What Chapters 1 to 3 establish

- The date is March fifth, twenty-one ten. The night runs from almost eleven o'clock
  through the activation and its immediate aftermath.
- The island is in the Atlantic Ocean, roughly fifty-six kilometers across, about
  thirty-five miles from one side to the other. Most of it is undeveloped wilderness.
- Near its center is a research settlement of laboratories, homes, workshops, medical
  facilities and other buildings, supporting nearly five hundred people.
- TOMBS, the Temporal Object Manipulation and Boundary System, also called the TOMBS
  Array, is the project Dr. Jack Bennett administers.
- Someone reached TOMBS through the network, revoked Jack's administrator credentials,
  ran the system, acquired the developed settlement as a boundary target, and
  activated it. Who, and how, and why, are open.
- TOMBS drew fourteen and then fifteen megawatts, kept running after the island grid
  was disconnected, and restarted after the physical shutdown lever was pulled. Where
  the power came from is open.
- The activation reduced everything inside the boundary. The boundary line traced the
  research district, housing blocks, medical center, workshops, utilities and the
  streets connecting them, and stopped almost perfectly where the developed town gave
  way to untouched island.
- Afterward the settlement's internal systems work and all external communication is
  gone. No satellite. No radio. Nothing beyond the settlement.
- Outside the developed zone, grass towers over buildings, a tree trunk will not fit
  on a camera frame, and a bead of water reads as a curved glass wall.
- A faint deep vibration passed through the building twice after the activation,
  softer than the array and more like distant thunder, with no seismic event on the
  structural monitor. Unexplained.

## The cast, as established

- **Dr. Jack Bennett** — TOMBS project administrator. Point of view for Chapters 1 to
  3.
- **Sarah Bennett** — scientist, Jack's wife, thirty-two weeks pregnant with a boy.
- **Lena Ortiz** — night-shift power technician at the island utility station. Present
  by wrist-terminal call only. She is not in the laboratory.
- **TOMBS** — speaks in system readouts, which are narrated.

## Where the story goes

1. **Chapters 1 to 3, done.** The catastrophe.
2. **Chapters 4 to 6, next.** Immediate aftermath: survival, community response,
   adaptation, consequences.
3. **Then, once the aftermath is established,** a time jump of about eighteen years.
4. **After the jump,** Jack and Sarah's son, about eighteen, becomes the primary
   protagonist. He was born after the catastrophe and has never experienced normal
   human scale. Academy and survival-training material belongs here.

We write about three connected chapters at a time. There is no fifty-chapter outline
and one should not be created. The pre-reboot plan is archived and non-canon.
