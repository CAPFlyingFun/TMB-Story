# Locations

Only what the approved manuscript establishes. Cite the chapter.

## The island

- In the Atlantic Ocean, remote. Roughly fifty-six kilometers across, about
  thirty-five miles from one side to the other `[ch 1]`.
- Most of it is undeveloped wilderness `[ch 1]`.
- **The island was not affected by the activation.** Its wilderness, its ocean and the
  rest of the world are exactly the size they were `[ch 3]`.

## The research settlement

- Near the island's center. Laboratories, homes, workshops, medical facilities and
  other buildings, supporting nearly five hundred people `[ch 1]`.
- Named features on the page: the research district, housing blocks, the medical
  center, workshops, utilities, the streets connecting them, a community center, and
  a communications building with a camera mounted high on it `[ch 2, ch 3]`.
- **The settlement is what was reduced.** The boundary traced the developed sections
  and stopped almost perfectly where the developed town gave way to untouched island
  `[ch 2, ch 3]`.
- After the activation the streets, the buildings, the streetlights and a parked
  utility vehicle are all exactly where they were and look normal from inside `[ch 3]`.

## Jack's laboratory

- Deep inside one of the settlement's laboratories `[ch 1]`. A workstation with a
  monitor, a keyboard, more than one workstation in the room, a terminal lock, an
  intercom to Sarah, and a door that slides `[ch 1]`.

## The main control room and the array chamber

- Reached along a corridor from the laboratory `[ch 2]`.
- A reinforced window looks into the chamber beyond. At its center stands the TOMBS
  Array `[ch 2]`.
- The control room has a primary console, a secondary console, emitter controls, an
  emergency panel with a physical shutdown lever, camera controls for the settlement
  perimeter feeds, a structural monitor, and emergency lighting `[ch 2, ch 3]`.

## The island utility station

- Where Lena Ortiz works the night shift. Reached by wrist-terminal call. It holds the
  island grid controls, sees building-level power draw, and can cut external access to
  the laboratory `[ch 2]`.
- Nobody from the laboratory goes there on the page.

## Beyond the developed zone, after the activation

- Grass stems tower above buildings, thousands of them disappearing into the night
  like a forest `[ch 3]`.
- The northern edge is buried beneath towering grass. The western road ends at an
  enormous wall of earth and vegetation. A tree Jack had walked past hundreds of times
  will not fit its trunk on a camera frame `[ch 3]`.
- A security light near the southern boundary showed a curved glass wall that turned
  out to be a bead of water `[ch 3]`.
- Rotating a high camera toward the coastline never finds the horizon. Grass stems,
  leaves and dark walls of soil fill the view in every direction `[ch 3]`.
