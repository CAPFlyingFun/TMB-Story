## Who knows what, at the end of Chapter 3

- **Jack:** that TOMBS finished rather than failed; that the boundary took the
  developed settlement and everyone in it; that the island did not change. He has no
  explanation for the intrusion, the power or the vibration.
- **Sarah:** the same. She is the one who says "They're running it" and "It took all
  of us" out loud.
- **Lena:** that there was a massive draw, that disconnecting did not stop it, and
  that nothing beyond the settlement can be reached. She has not been told what the
  boundary was.
- **The audience:** everything Jack and Sarah have, and no more. Nobody has identified
  the intruder.

## What the author knows and no character does

`WORLD_RULES.md` records hidden truths that sit under these questions. **Nothing in a
chapter may answer, hint at or edge toward them until Joshua chooses to.** In short:
the edge is not a wall but the inner edge of a scale-transition region, TOMBS holds
nothing in place, and the island itself was transformed relative to Earth. None of
that closes a question above. Q03 (the array's power) and Q07 (external
communications) stay open.

## Deliberately unassigned

The year the settlement was founded, and anything about the island's owners or a
concealment system (`WORLD_RULES.md` section 6). Do not invent them. The scale factor
now has an author value of about 1:180 island-relative, but no character has said it
aloud, so Q05 stays open.

## Who knows what, at the end of Chapter 10 (approved 2026-10-27)

- **Jack, Sarah, Lena and Mark:** the boundary file, its three-week-old date, PHASE ONE READY,
  the tablet at the north pumping station and the lost groundskeeper's badge. Whoever did this
  planned for that trail being found.
- **Doctor Mercer and the department heads:** that the town got smaller (Ch 6, Ch 10). Not the
  file, the tablet or the badge.
- **Aiden:** that there is a giant spider outside the greenhouse.
- **The rest of the settlement:** finding out on its own at dawn.
