# World Rules

**Author-level physics and geography for TMB. Joshua, 2026-09-25 (Decision 0025).**

Read this before writing or reviewing any chapter. It describes how the world
actually works. The characters know only part of it, and that gap is deliberate.
Every rule below is tagged:

- **[KNOWN]** the characters know it, or would know it by living there. It may
  appear on the page when a scene needs it.
- **[HIDDEN]** author knowledge only. No character says it, deduces it or gets
  close to it, and no narration hints at it, until Joshua chooses the chapter.
  The reason it is written down is so that nothing on the page ever contradicts it.
- **[NOT CANON]** a candidate idea. Do not establish it, name it or imply it
  without his approval.

This is speculative science fiction. It does not have to obey real physics, but it
does have to obey itself. When a chapter touches scale, geometry, power or signals,
check it against this file.

---

## 1. What TOMBS does [HIDDEN]

TOMBS does not shrink objects inside an ordinary fixed space. It performs a
**one-time spatial-scale transformation**:

    FULLY TRANSFORMED SETTLEMENT
      -> gradual scale-transition region
        -> normal island-relative environment

- **The developed settlement is transformed uniformly** to about 1:180 relative to
  the surrounding island. Everything inside keeps its proportions: streets,
  buildings and the distances between them are the same to the people inside as
  they were before. The town does not collapse into a point, does not leave a huge
  empty footprint where it stood, and does not keep its buildings kilometers apart
  relative to its own shrunken people.
- **A transition region surrounds it.** Spatial scale changes smoothly across it
  until it rejoins the island's geometry. That is what joins a 1:180 town to a 1:1
  island without a seam.
- **TOMBS fires once and shuts down.** It does not hold the result in place. There
  is no powered field, no dome and no fishbowl. The new scale relationship is
  permanent unless someone deliberately reverses or alters it.
- **The characters do not understand any of this.** They take the visible edge of
  the transformed settlement, where the pavement stops, to be "the TOMBS boundary",
  and the manuscript uses that word for it. That stays: it is what they believe.
  Later investigation may find a larger transition envelope beyond the apparent
  boundary. Not in the next chapter, and not until Joshua says.

**Consistent with the page as written:** Chapter 3's activation (the laboratory
"seemed to stretch and then compress", light bent) reads as space being
transformed. TOMBS went offline "because it finished" (Ch 3), which is the one-shot
rule. And at the edge in Chapter 5: "There was no glowing wall, no distortion, no
lingering TOMBS field. Asphalt simply stopped and soil began."

## 2. The second scale layer [HIDDEN]

The transformation is nested, and only the inner layer is known:

| relationship | scale | who knows |
|---|---|---|
| Earth to the whole artificial island | about 1:180 | nobody in the story |
| island to the settlement and its people | about 1:180 | the characters, as "the town shrank" |
| Earth to the settlement and its people | about 1:32,400 | nobody |

- **Everything on the page uses the island-relative scale only.** An adult is about
  seven to ten millimeters against island objects (Ch 6: "somewhere around several
  millimeters tall"). Grass towers, insects and spiders are enormous, birds are
  huge, twigs behave like logs, and droplets and terrain become obstacles.
  `reference/README.md` is the 1:180 chart for that.
- **Chapter 3's ending is deliberately wrong:** "Their town had shrunk. / And the
  island had not." That is Jack and Sarah's reasonable conclusion, and the reader
  shares it. So does "The island hadn't grown. The wilderness hadn't changed. The
  ocean hadn't changed." No chapter corrects it until Joshua reveals the outer layer.
- **Derived, author-only arithmetic,** worked out here so that nobody works it out
  differently later: fifty-six kilometers divided by 180 is about 311 meters, so
  the island is about that size relative to Earth. A 1.75 m adult divided by 32,400
  is about 54 micrometers relative to Earth.

## 3. The artificial island

- **[KNOWN, not yet said on the page]** The island is man-made. It is a massive
  research island about fifty-six kilometers across before the event. The page so
  far says only "a remote island in the Atlantic". Its people live on it and know
  what it is, so saying so later is not a reveal.
- **[KNOWN in principle]** It has extensive engineered infrastructure inside it.
  Possibilities, none yet on the page: maintenance tunnels, engineering decks,
  utility corridors, ballast compartments, pump rooms, water systems, storm
  shelters, sealed structural compartments, power distribution, communications
  infrastructure and emergency facilities. Which of these exist, and where, is
  decided when a chapter needs one. Do not map it all in advance.

## 4. Power [KNOWN]

- The settlement has **its own local microgrid and backup systems**. Normally it
  also connects to a larger **island-wide master grid**.
- That is why the transformed settlement still has power after it is cut off.
  Chapter 4 fits this: three localized faults, most of the developed zone still
  powered, and the power conduit found cut cleanly at the boundary.
- **This does not answer Q03.** Where TOMBS drew its power after being disconnected
  (Ch 2) is still open. The microgrid explains the town, not the array.

## 5. Communications [KNOWN]

- **Local systems work:** Wi-Fi and networking, local servers, handheld and
  security radios, cameras, emergency channels and internal systems.
- **External communications do not.** Nothing beyond the settlement answers
  (Ch 3, Q07). Why is still open.

## 6. Signature management [NOT CANON]

A possible future element, and not established. The island may carry a
sophisticated signature-management or concealment system because of military,
government, contractor or advanced-research involvement. It would not be magical
invisibility: radar detection, electronic countermeasures, false returns, emission
control and sensor networks are the kind of thing meant. It might have activated
during the TOMBS incident without Jack and Sarah knowing.

Do not establish the system, its owner or its link to Q07 without Joshua's approval.

## 7. Questions the rules leave open (for Joshua, not for a chapter)

These came up while recording the rules. Nothing in Chapters 1 to 6 needs to change
for any of them, but the first chapter that goes beyond the edge will.

- **Where the transition region starts.** Chapter 5 puts full island-scale material
  right at the pavement line. The grass base is "wider than his torso", the
  fingernail clipping is "longer than Jack was tall", and a droplet rises "nearly
  to Jack's chest". Those are exactly 1:180 against island objects. If the scale
  gradient began at the pavement, the nearest grass would look close to normal
  size. Either the transition changes distances rather than the size of what stands
  in it, or it begins somewhere Jack has not yet reached. The first fits the page
  as written; the choice is Joshua's.
- **When and how the island's own transformation happened.** "Fifty-six kilometers
  across before the event" suggests the outer layer happened the same night. Whether
  it has its own transition region out into the ocean is not stated.
