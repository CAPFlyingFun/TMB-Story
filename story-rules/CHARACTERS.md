## Dr. Jack Bennett

- **Role:** TOMBS project administrator. Point of view for Chapters 1 to 3.
- **Established:** the only person who can revoke his own credentials, and he did not
  revoke them `[ch 1]`. Asleep at his terminal when the alarm went `[ch 1]`. Wears a
  wrist terminal he calls the island utility station on `[ch 2]`.
- **Voice:** short, dry, deflects with a joke and then does the work. "No. That's why
  I'm calling the smarter scientist." "I was reviewing them internally." "Tonight's
  developing a theme." He answers a hard question with one word when he has one, and
  says "I don't know" when he does not.
- **Under pressure:** he reaches for Sarah before anything else. He pulled the
  physical lever when the software would not answer. He changed a full evacuation
  order to a shelter order the moment he realized he did not know which side of the
  boundary was safe `[ch 2]`.
- **Knows at the end of Chapter 3:** TOMBS finished rather than failed; the settlement
  and everyone in it were reduced; the island was not.

## Sarah Bennett

- **Role:** scientist. Jack's wife. Thirty-two weeks pregnant with a boy `[ch 2, ch 3]`.
- **Established:** carries a tablet `[ch 1]`. Jack calls her "the smarter scientist"
  and she takes the keyboard off him `[ch 1]`. "Being thirty-two weeks pregnant does
  not mean made of glass" `[ch 2]`. The baby is a he: "He moved" `[ch 3]`, "you're his
  father" `[ch 2]`. Lately the baby treats eleven at night as morning `[ch 1]`.
- **Voice:** precise, fast, dry, and she wins exchanges by asking the next question.
  "Define weird." "Too clean." "Maybe good, or maybe everyone-inside-the-field-dies
  bad?" "Apparently TOMBS disagrees."
- **Under pressure:** she reads the numbers twice before she says them, and she says
  the conclusion out loud when Jack will not. "They're running it." "It took all of
  us."

## Lena Ortiz

- **Role:** night-shift power technician at the island utility station.
- **Established:** reached only through Jack's wrist terminal in Chapters 2 to 4. She is NOT
  in the laboratory then `[ch 2, ch 3]`. From Chapter 5 she is in the control room in person,
  in her orange utility jacket, having locked the utility station `[ch 5]`. She was about to call Jack when he called her. She
  reported fourteen then fifteen megawatts, cut external access, disconnected TOMBS
  from the island grid, and confirmed the disconnection had not stopped it. She ran
  the settlement-wide emergency order Jack gave her, and after the activation reported
  that internal systems were mostly responding while everything beyond the settlement
  was unreachable.
- **Voice:** direct, procedural, no padding. "Doing it now." "That's impossible."
  "What level?" "Nothing."

## Jack and Sarah's son

- **Established:** unborn at the end of Chapter 3, at thirty-two weeks, alive and
  moving after the activation. He is inside the boundary when it fires.
- **Planned:** he becomes the primary protagonist after the time jump of about
  eighteen years. He was born after the catastrophe and has never experienced normal
  human scale. He has no name on the page yet.

## Mark Jones

- **Role:** perimeter security; on the radio as Unit Twelve `[ch 4, ch 5]`.
- **Established:** checks the southern boundary with Jack, from inside the developed zone
  `[ch 5]`; reports the dinner-plate mark `[ch 8]`; walks out to the eastern marker with Jack
  and offers the door-camera footage `[ch 9]`; starts telling the department heads, medical
  first `[ch 10]`. One of the four who know about the file, the tablet and the badge.
- **Voice:** practical, dry, a little deadpan. "UFO?" "That is deeply unpleasant."

## Doctor Mercer

- **Role:** the settlement's doctor, at the medical center.
- **Established:** calls the control room before dawn to ask whether the lab caused the
  vibration, and is told the town got smaller `[ch 6]`; calls again with two more patients
  awake and her patience nearly gone `[ch 8]`. Told first among the department heads
  `[ch 10]`.
- **Voice:** brisk, procedural once she has the facts. "What do you need from medical?"

## Aiden

- **Role:** works at the greenhouse `[ch 10]`.
- **Established:** calls the facilities channel at dawn, breathless, about a spider the size
  of a delivery truck outside the greenhouse; told to stay where he is `[ch 10]`.
