## TOMBS

- **Temporal Object Manipulation and Boundary System** `[ch 2]`. Also called **the
  TOMBS Array** `[ch 2]`. Jack Bennett is its project administrator.
- **Form:** several articulated rings surrounding a central platform, in a chamber
  behind a reinforced window `[ch 2]`.
- **Normal operation:** the rings position themselves around a target while the system
  establishes a controlled boundary `[ch 2]`.
- **Designed for:** controlled objects, laboratory-scale targets. Tested on equipment,
  vehicles and biological samples. "It's never created anything remotely that large"
  `[ch 2]`.
- **Power:** drew fourteen and then fifteen megawatts on the night. Yesterday's full
  test did not use half that `[ch 2]`.
- **Unexplained:** it kept running after Lena disconnected it from the island grid. The
  backup capacitors are not enough for that draw and the internal reactor was offline.
  Where the power came from is an open question `[ch 2]`.
- **Unexplained:** Jack pulled the physical shutdown lever and cut the physical
  connection. The rings stopped, then started again `[ch 2]`.
- **The event:** every light went white, sound ceased entirely, the laboratory seemed
  to stretch and then compress, light bent around Sarah's body, the floor seemed to go,
  and then everything snapped back `[ch 3]`.
- **After:** TOMBS went offline "because it finished", not because it was stopped. The
  consoles were intact, the array had stopped, the emergency lighting was normal, and
  the sensor grid read normal for temperature, pressure, atmosphere and structure
  `[ch 3]`.
- **The scale factor** was calculated, locked and recorded. Its value is displayed on
  the page and never spoken. **No number has been assigned. Do not invent one.**

## The machine voice

TOMBS and the settlement's systems speak, and their lines are narrated in the
audiobook. The manuscript's style, preserved verbatim on import:

- Sentence case, short, clipped, no speaker attribution.
- The system writes the project name as "Tombs", while the prose writes "TOMBS".
- Both "Warning." messages are followed by a lower-case word.
- A readout sits in the same paragraph as the reaction it causes, never as a heading.

The sixteen readouts in Chapters 1 to 3, in order: "Warning. unauthorized system
access." · "Security protocol violation." · "Tombs project." · "Boundary control." ·
"Tombs array remote initialization request." · "Request denied." · "Request denied." ·
"Access revoked." · "Warning. array power above standby threshold." · "Boundary
acquisition in progress." · "Boundary acquired." · "Scale factor calculating." ·
"Access denied." · "Access denied." · "Access denied." · "Scale factor locked." ·
"Boundary event complete."
